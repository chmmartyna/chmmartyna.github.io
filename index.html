About

I work in AI governance and regulatory compliance, helping organizations navigate a fast-moving legal landscape across the EU, US, and Asia. My interest sits at the intersection of digital policy and geopolitics, focusing on how public and private actors cooperate to build technology that serves people while preserving fundamental rights and sovereignty.

My background moved from a broad foundation in political science at Leiden University to applied digital public policy at Sciences Po, and now to hands-on AI governance and compliance work. Along the way I've gained experience across consulting, think tanks, national administrations, and intergovernmental organizations, which gave me a truly multidisciplinary, international outlook on how regulation actually gets implemented.

I believe that governance and compliance work delivers the most value when it feeds back into how policy itself is shaped, where implementation realities inform the decisions made upstream. AI's transformative potential and its risks need to be addressed together, not traded off, and that principle continues to orient my work: staying close to policy design, and building governance that holds up in practice.
